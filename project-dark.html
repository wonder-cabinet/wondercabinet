Wonder Cabinet ★★ مجلس العجب
Laurence Sammour, Sary Moussa, Abed Kobeissy  Makram Aboul Hosn
Bethlehem

This electro-acoustic work was created during Christmas 2023, when Bethlehem's Greek Orthodox Church of the Nativity chose to cancel all festive celebrations in response to the era-defining ethnic cleansing taking place in Palestine.

In this context, Wonder Cabinet, commissioned a new piece that brought together Lawrence Sammour—head of the Church of the Nativity's choir for over fifty years—with Beirut-based artists and musicians Sary Moussa and Abed Kobeissy.

Blending sacred choral tradition with experimental electronic music, the work was broadcasted on Christmas Eve 2023, with a re-run on December 27th, via Radio Al-Hara and more than 50 radio stations worldwide, including NTS Radio, Lyl Radio, Radio Flouka, Noods Radio, Radio Atheer, Radio Refuge Worldwide, Stegi Radio, Cashmere Radio, Root Radio, Radio Nopal, and Radio Raheem Milano.

A year later, the project had its first live premiere at Le Guess Who? Festival in Utrecht, Netherlands, marking the first in-person meeting of the three collaborators, who had completed the work remotely due to travel restrictions between Palestine and Lebanon. The 32-minute live version was performed together for the first time during the festival.

In December 2025, Wonder Cabinet, Radio alHara and the artists presented the work again in Beirut as part of the Beirut and Beyond International Music Festival along with musician Makram Aboul Hosn.
