Composer · Sound Artist · Vocalist / Cairo
ملحّنة · فنانة صوتية · مغنية / القاهرة

Yara Mekawei is an Egyptian composer, sound artist, and vocalist whose practice moves between electronic music, field recording, and the living forms of Arabic vocal tradition. Her month-long residency at the Wonder Cabinet in November 2025 is her first extended period of work in Palestine.

يارا مكاوي ملحّنةٌ مصريةٌ وفنانةٌ صوتيةٌ ومغنيةٌ تتنقّل ممارستُها بين الموسيقى الإلكترونية والتسجيل الميداني والأشكال الحيّة للتقليد الصوتي العربي. إقامتُها لمدّة شهرٍ في مجلس العجب في نوفمبر ٢٠٢٥ هي أوّل فترةٍ موسّعةٍ لها في فلسطين.

Her work often begins with listening — an extended, patient attention to the specific acoustic conditions of a place. In Bethlehem, she is particularly interested in what she calls the "layered time" of the city's soundscape: the overlapping of the call to prayer and church bells; the sound of stone-cutting from the quarries on the hill; the silence of alleyways in the early morning, broken only by birds and distant traffic.

During the residency, Mekawei will be developing a new body of work for voice, electronics, and field recordings — pieces that use the sonic material of Bethlehem as a compositional resource without reducing it to a backdrop or a spectacle.

She will work in the Wonder Cabinet's ground floor studio, with access to the space at all hours. She has asked for the residency to be largely private — a period of concentration rather than a public process.

The residency will conclude with a single public event: an evening performance at the Wonder Cabinet in which Mekawei will present works developed during the month. The programme will include new compositions, improvisations, and a listening session using recordings made across the city.

Mekawei has released four albums, most recently Ard (2024), described as "a meditation on belonging, soil, and the voice as a cartographic instrument." She has performed at Haus der Kulturen der Welt (Berlin), the Cairo Jazz Festival, and L'Institut du Monde Arabe (Paris). She studied composition at the Cairo Conservatoire and electronic music at IRCAM.

The Wonder Cabinet residency programme supports artists working at the intersection of sound, space, and cultural memory. Residencies are offered for periods of two weeks to two months, with accommodation, studio access, and a production budget.

"I am not interested in documenting a place. I am interested in what the place does to the ear — and what the ear, in attending, does to the place."

«لا يعنيني توثيقُ مكانٍ. يعنيني ما يفعلُه المكانُ بالأذن — وما تفعله الأذنُ، حين تُصغي، بالمكان.»

— Yara Mekawei

The Wonder Cabinet residency programme was established in 2024 to support artists, researchers, and practitioners working in the cultural fields that the space exists to serve: sound and music, architecture and design, the visual arts, and the literature and oral traditions of the Arab world.

Residents are selected through an open call and through direct invitation. The programme welcomes applications from artists based in Palestine and from the broader Arab world. International applications are considered on a case-by-case basis, with preference given to those with established relationships to the region.

The residency programme is currently funded through a combination of the Wonder Cabinet's operational budget and a small number of individual patrons. We are seeking additional support to expand the number of residencies we can offer each year and to increase the production budgets available to residents.

If you are interested in supporting the residency programme, or in applying for a future residency, contact us at office@wondercabinet.space.
