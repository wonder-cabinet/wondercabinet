Annual Project مشروع سنوي First Edition · 24 May – 2 June 2024 الطبعة الأولى · ٢٤ مايو – ٢ يونيو ٢٠٢٤ Cremisan Valley, Bethlehem وادي كريميزان، بيت لحم

Sounds
of Places

أصوات
الأماكن

An annual residency-based sound gathering dedicated to the sonic identity of places — conceived by Elias and Yousef Anastas, rooted in the Wonder Cabinet in Bethlehem.

تجمّعٌ صوتيٌّ سنويٌّ قائمٌ على الإقامة، مكرّسٌ للهوية الصوتية للأماكن — من تصوّر إلياس ويوسف أناستاس، متجذّرٌ في مجلس العجب في بيت لحم.

Sounds of Places was launched in the spring of 2024, amid the ongoing genocide in Gaza and the increasingly restrictive annexation practices in the West Bank. The project positions itself as a tool of resistance, protection, and preservation — attending to places that are at risk of becoming inaccessible to their legitimate inhabitants.

انطلق «أصوات الأماكن» في ربيع ٢٠٢٤، وسط الإبادة الجماعية المستمرة في غزة والممارسات الاستيطانية المتصاعدة في الضفة الغربية. يضع المشروعُ نفسه أداةً للمقاومة والحماية والحفاظ — إذ يُعنى بالأماكن المهدّدة بالأقتال عن أصحابها الشرعيين.

The first edition took place in the Cremisan Valley — one of the last green spaces around Bethlehem, a place of extraordinary biodiversity and deep historical significance, threatened by the ongoing construction of Israel's separation barrier.

Over ten days, the residency immersed participants in the valley to narrate and preserve its character: its sounds, its plants, its soil, its quality of light and air at different hours of the day. The programme involved sound artists, craftspeople, designers, visual artists, and the nuns of the Cremisan Monastery — a Salesian community that has tended the valley for over a century.

Alongside workshops, conversations, communal meals, and healing activities, three sound sculptures were installed permanently in the landscape, designed to be activated by wind, rain, humidity, dew, and sun — and by the presence of visitors.

The project was developed in the first months of 2024 at the Wonder Cabinet, with Ibrahim Owais — Palestinian musician and co-founder of Radio alHara — as artistic producer, and Ilaria Speri as project coordinator.

The three sculptures resonated with the valley and with each other, creating a living repository of sounds that spoke to the spirits of other environments — close by and on the other side of the world. The work refuses the logic of documentation as extraction: the sounds are not removed from the valley but deposited back into it, altered by encounter.

Sounds of Places also traveled internationally. An installation and performance edition was presented at Triennale Milano in October 2025 (Spazio VOCE) and at Le Guess Who? 2024 festival.

"A tool of resistance, protection, and preservation of places at risk of becoming inaccessible to their legitimate inhabitants."

«أداةٌ للمقاومة والحماية والحفاظ على الأماكن المهدّدة بالانتزاع من أصحابها الشرعيين.»

Newton Gaza (Jad Atoui) — A sound sculpture dedicated to Hussam El Attar, a boy in Gaza who built wind turbines from scrap to generate electricity for his family during the blockade. The sculpture transforms the wind of the Cremisan Valley into an act of solidarity across distance.

Cremisan Herbarium (Sofia Lambrou & Nisreen Tahhan, with Areej Ashhab, Sireen Alawi, Majdi Hadid) — A work that weaves sound, movement, photography, and ecology to capture the valley's essence. Using cyanotype printing and field recording, participants documented the plants and sounds of the landscape, creating passport-shaped herbariums: a form of identity document for a threatened place.

The project grew into a collective field recording session that drew on the landscape across a wide radius — from the valley itself to the King Hussein Crossing into Jordan — conducted by P KIRN and Sofia Lambrou together with the wider Sounds of Places crew.

The recordings from the first edition have been broadcast internationally via Radio alHara and were featured in a special programme produced with Refuge Worldwide. They constitute the beginning of a living archive — to be continued in future editions, in other threatened landscapes.
